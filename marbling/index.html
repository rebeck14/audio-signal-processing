Ink on Water 墨流し

Tap to drop. Drag to stir. Return for fresh water.

Closed-form marbling after Jaffer: every drop pushes the boundaries already on the water outward by √(1 + r²/d²), a rake bends them with a tine line, and the stick drags a Gaussian core with a counter-rotating wake. The picture is vectors the whole time, so the SVG is exact.
