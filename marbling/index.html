Ink on Water 墨流し

Tap to drop. Hold to pour. Drag to stir. ⌘ and move for size, ⌘⌥ and move to turn the rake or shape. Scroll works too. Drop an SVG on the water to make it ink.

Sumi ink is a surfactant. Where it lands, surface tension drops, and the surface is pulled toward clean water: that Marangoni pull is what spreads a drop and pushes earlier rings outward. The bath here is a surface flow with that pull, floor drag set by depth, viscosity, and a stick that drags water with it. Ink is carried by the flow, never painted.
